chapter four

On the evening itself

A little plant stake at each table opens these pages on your phone. Four things to do between supper and dessert:

A page for his story

Write him one page — a wish, a memory, a piece of advice. Every page becomes a printed book he'll grow up reading.

The predictions ledger

Birth date, weight, hair, first word. Sealed at the end of the night — opened only when he arrives.

The photo drop

Everyone's photos, one shared album — looping on the screen by the dessert table while the evening happens.

The recording nook

Twenty seconds of your voice, saved for him. Tell him what August smelled like the night before he was famous.

epilogue

This book doesn't end at the party

When he arrives in October, the predictions unseal, the library becomes his reading list — and these pages quietly become his family's own. Each milestone plants something new in the garden.
