My recommendation: watercolor & ink, with etched accents

Watercolor & ink carries the site — it's exactly the hand of your three mood boards, it flatters the real photography (the baby photos, the eventual garden photos), and it ages into the family-site years without ever feeling like a party flyer.

Botanical etching becomes the supporting voice: chapter frames, dividers, the plate-style captions, the printed keepsake. Print grain from the vintage style gets applied site-wide at 5% so every page feels like paper, not pixels.
