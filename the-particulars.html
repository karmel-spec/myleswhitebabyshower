Chapter one

A new chapter begins

A garden inspired by stories and the wonder of childhood, where imagination takes root and love helps it grow. One long gingham table under the string lights, lanterns down the middle, daisies in terracotta.

When

Saturday, August 15th, 2026, at 7 in the evening.

Supper first, games while the light goes gold, dessert under the string lights.

Where

Among the terracotta pots and greenhouse plants at Grandma's garden.

128 Westview Circle, Orem, Utah.

look for the blue gingham tables

What to bring

Mostly: yourself. Beyond that, we love the tradition of a children's book instead of a card — and all books are welcome: new, well worn and loved, and everything in between. Write a note inside the front cover so he always knows it came from you.

If you'd like to help him grow, his registries are in chapter four.
