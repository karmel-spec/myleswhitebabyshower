The Dressing Derby

One baby doll. One tiny outfit. One merciless stopwatch. Undress and re-dress the doll — the hat, the baby mittens, and both socks all come off and go back on — and the clock runs from the moment you first touch him until the last snap is snapped. Come back for another attempt any time tonight. Only your best time counts, but the leaderboard remembers how many tries it took.
