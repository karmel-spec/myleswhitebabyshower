The Story Garden

the dressing derby

One doll · one outfit · one merciless stopwatch

Undress, re-dress,
beat the clock

the snaps have opinions

Hat, baby mittens, and both socks — all off, all back on,
first touch to last snap, while the clock runs.
Time yourself from the party page on your phone and log your run —
only your best time counts, but the leaderboard remembers your tries.
